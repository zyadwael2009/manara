## Full description (4000 chars)

Manara is a full K-12 learning management app for schools that want
students, teachers, parents, and administrators on one platform.

**Student home**
- See today's classes, homework, and quizzes at a glance
- Track lesson progress, attendance, and course averages
- Earn certificates when you complete a course
- Get a diploma at graduation
- View your standards mastery per subject
- Manage your fees in-app

**Teacher tools**
- Class + course rosters
- Live gradebook with rubric-based categories
- Quiz authoring with multiple-choice, true/false, short answer, and
  fill-in-the-blank questions
- Assignment inbox with inline grading and feedback
- Group assignments with shared submissions and one-tap grade fan-out
- Attendance-taking with one-tap "mark all present"
- Weekly timetable with optional Meet/Zoom links per period

**Parent portal**
- See your child's classes, grades, attendance, and fees
- Download PDF report cards and transcripts
- Message teachers directly
- Get real-time notifications when new grades are posted

**Admin panel**
- School-wide dashboards: attendance patterns, cohort comparison,
  outstanding fees
- Sections, grades, and classes with drag-and-drop student placement
- Bulk CSV import for users
- Grading scale + rubric configuration
- Bulk graduate a Grade-12 class, which auto-issues diplomas
- Curriculum standards library with per-standard mastery rollups

**Everything else**
- Bell notifications with per-category opt-out
- Global search across courses, lessons, quizzes, and students
- ICS calendar feed you can subscribe to from Google Calendar or
  Apple Calendar
- Report card, transcript, certificate, diploma, and fee statement
  PDFs — all downloadable
- Announcements you can broadcast school-wide, class-wide, or
  course-wide

**Signed-in only**
Manara is deployed by your school — accounts are created by the school
admin. You'll receive credentials from your school office.
